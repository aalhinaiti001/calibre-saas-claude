# Guardrails

Version 1.0. These are product rules and client obligations. They come from the July 2026 deep research assessment and apply to every form of Calibre: manual, kit, or software. Local counsel reviews this file before the first paid Verdict in each market.

## 1. Allowed uses

Calibre may be used to:

- define a finance role's standard before candidates are reviewed
- structure how a panel reads CVs, interviews finalists, and records evidence
- make panelists' disagreements visible and resolve them with written reasons
- produce a written record of a hiring decision and the reasoning behind it
- follow up on the outcome of that decision at fixed intervals

## 2. Prohibited uses

Calibre must not be used to:

- screen or rank applicants before a shortlist exists
- reject any candidate automatically or on the basis of a score alone
- produce an overall "fit" score, a pass mark, or a hire or reject label
- override demonstrated, job related competence without a written job related reason
- assess personality, work style, culture fit, health, disability, age, family status, nationality beyond right to work, religion, or any protected characteristic
- administer any test, questionnaire, or exercise to candidates through Calibre
- feed candidate data to any automated or model based scoring, ranking, or summarisation
- make decisions about existing employees: promotion, performance, redundancy

The organisation owner accepts these terms before a workspace is created. Breach is grounds for termination of the licence and deletion of the workspace.

## 3. Candidate notice

Before a finalist is entered, the employer tells the candidate, in writing, in a form they can keep:

> As part of our final selection for [role], a panel of [n] people will each assess you independently against a written set of criteria for the role, then compare their assessments and record the reasons for the final decision. This is a structured interview and review process. It is not a test and does not involve any personality or psychometric assessment. Your name, CV and the panel's notes are held by [employer] and, if we use the Calibre workspace, by Daftar Advisory as our processor, for [90] days after the decision, after which your personal details are deleted and only anonymised scores are kept. You may ask to see the criteria at any time, ask for reasonable adjustments to the interview process, and contact [name, email] with any question or objection.

The notice is a template. Counsel adapts it per market. The employer, not Calibre, is responsible for sending it. The kit and the app record whether it was sent.

## 4. Reasonable adjustments

Any candidate may request an adjustment to how they are interviewed or assessed. The hiring lead records the request and the adjustment made. Adjustments never appear in the rubric, the scores, or the memo, except as a note that the process was adjusted, without the reason.

## 5. Data handling

| Data | Where it lives | How long | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finalist name, contact, CV | Finalist record and file store | Until retention purge, default 90 days after issue | Panel seats for that role, and the organisation owner |
| Scores and evidence notes | Score records | Indefinitely, tied to initials only after purge | Own scores: the panelist. All scores: the panel, after reveal. |
| Calibrations, verdict, outcomes | Role records | Indefinitely, initials only | Panel seats and owner |
| Audit log | Append only table | Indefinitely | Owner, and Daftar on request for a dispute |

Region: EU (Frankfurt) by default for Jordan and UAE clients, pending counsel. Any client requiring regional hosting is served from a separately deployed instance. No cross organisation queries exist in the product.

## 6. Roles and responsibilities

- **Employer (organisation owner).** Data controller. Sends the candidate notice, handles access and deletion requests, makes the hiring decision.
- **Daftar Advisory.** Data processor when the workspace is used. Never a decision maker. Contractual terms allocate this explicitly.
- **Panelists.** Score on their own evidence, keep confidentiality, do not discuss finalists before reveal.

## 7. Claims discipline

Calibre materials, the memo included, never state or imply that the method:

- has been validated as a predictor of job performance
- reduces legal risk
- measures personality, potential, or fit
- is endorsed by any professional body

The only claim made is the one that is true: the method makes panel judgment explicit, comparable, and recorded. Any statistic quoted in Calibre material carries its source.

## 8. Incident rule

Any credible complaint that a Calibre Verdict was used in a prohibited way, or any data incident involving finalist data, is reported to the founder within 24 hours, logged, and reviewed with counsel before the next Verdict in that organisation.

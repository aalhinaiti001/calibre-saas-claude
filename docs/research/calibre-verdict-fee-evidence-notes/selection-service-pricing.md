# Third-party candidate selection and assessment support: what employers pay (global and GCC, 2023-2026)

Research date: 5 Oct 2026. Purpose: place a USD 6,000 fixed fee per role (structured hiring advisory reviewing one shortlist of 2-6 finalists for one finance role, with a 2-5 person panel) against real alternatives.

**Conventions used throughout**
- Evidence type tags: **[LIST]** = price published by the seller on its own page or price sheet. **[PROCUREMENT]** = public-sector document (council report, framework price document, FOI). **[BUYER-DATA]** = aggregated transaction data (e.g. Vendr). **[3P-EST]** = third-party estimate or blog, often from a competitor. **[ANECDOTAL]** = reported in press or marketing copy without a primary document.
- FX rates. GBP->USD 1.34, the Sept 2026 average of 1.3409 per [exchangerates.org.uk](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/GBP-USD-spot-exchange-rates-history-2026.html). OFX gives a 6-month average of 1.3434 to Sept 2026 ([OFX](https://www.ofx.com/en-us/forex-news/historical-exchange-rates/gbp/usd/)). AED->USD uses the fixed peg of 3.6725 and SAR->USD the fixed peg of 3.75. I did not fetch a source page for either peg, but both are long-standing official pegs. VAT is excluded unless stated.
- "Accessed Oct 2026" means the page had no visible date and I read it in Oct 2026.

## 1. Executive / leadership assessment per candidate (Korn Ferry, SHL, Hogan, DDI, RRA, Egon Zehnder, Spencer Stuart, Mettl, Thomas), including GCC

### Takeaway
None of the big-brand firms (Korn Ferry, DDI, Russell Reynolds, Egon Zehnder, Spencer Stuart, Mercer|Mettl) publishes a per-candidate executive assessment price. The best hard numbers come from three places: public-sector documents (a UK council pays **£750 + VAT, about USD 1,005, per shortlisted senior candidate** for psychologist-reviewed leadership psychometrics in 2025-26), UK boutique list prices (about USD 500-1,300 per candidate for psychometrics plus a psychologist debrief), and Hogan resellers (about USD 160-1,600 per candidate depending on reports and debrief). Psychologist-led executive assessments with interviews and simulations are reported at **USD 2,000-3,000** at the low end, **USD 10,000-50,000+** for C-suite, and up to **USD 85,000** for CEO simulations. These last figures are mostly marketing or press claims, not price lists. GCC providers do publish some AED/SAR prices, which sit at about **USD 680-2,000 per candidate or position**.

### Inferences
- A psychologist-reviewed leadership assessment for a senior UK public-sector shortlist costs about USD 1,000 per finalist. For 4-6 finalists that is USD 4,000-6,000, the same order as the USD 6,000 fee. But that buys a psychometric read and a short report, not panel design, facilitation or decision support.
- Boutique "psychometrics + psychologist interview + report" packages cluster around USD 1,000-1,300 per candidate in the UK. GCC published prices are similar (USD 680-2,000 per candidate or position).
- Big-brand executive assessment, priced at USD 10,000+ per candidate, is only evidenced by marketing or press claims. Treat it as an upper-bound context, not a benchmark.

### Gaps
- I found no published per-candidate price for Korn Ferry, DDI, Russell Reynolds, Egon Zehnder, Spencer Stuart or Mercer|Mettl executive assessment. Mettl's pricing page is a quote form, checked 17 Sep 2026 per [AssessAll](https://www.assessall.com/alternatives/mercer-mettl).
- I found no GCC government tender (e.g. Etimad KSA, UAE federal portals) disclosing per-candidate assessment rates. These portals mostly need a login and were not reachable.
- The Performance Programs "Hogan Price Sheet Jan 2024" PDF and the SHL panel-feedback line prices were not extractable.
- I found no published DDI assessment-centre day rate.

## 2. Psychometric test pricing per candidate (comparison only; the service priced is NOT psychometric)

### Takeaway
Off-the-shelf psychometric or skills tests are cheap per candidate: **under USD 1 to about USD 50** on high-volume platforms, and **USD 150-400** for branded personality reports such as Thomas PPA or a single Hogan report. Most vendors sell annual subscriptions (about USD 2,600-20,000/yr) rather than per-candidate prices.

### Cited Findings
- AssessAll (India) publishes 1 credit = ₹30 = USD 0.50. A leadership battery is 45-50 credits, **USD 22.50-25 per candidate**. [LIST, Sep 2026] — [AssessAll](https://www.assessall.com/alternatives/mercer-mettl)
- Mercer|Mettl: no public price. A third party reports a Starter plan where "each additional attempt [costs] $15". [3P-EST, Dec 2025] — [PricingNow](https://pricingnow.com/question/mercer-mettl-online-assessment-platform-pricing/)
- TestGorilla Assessments: Free plan with 10 credits/month. Paid plans "**from $215/mo per account**". Skills tests cost 1 credit per candidate. [LIST, accessed Oct 2026] — [TestGorilla pricing](https://www.testgorilla.com/pricing/)
- Criteria Corp: Vendr median **USD 13,225/yr** (range USD 9,580-19,200). Pricing is by annual assessment volume, not seats. [BUYER-DATA, 2026] — [Vendr Criteria](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/criteria)
- Thomas International: per employee per month, with a minimum spend under 100 employees. Prices vary outside CA/US/UK. Numeric prices are not displayed in the extracted page. The Expert/Assess Accredited plan is price on application. [LIST, accessed Oct 2026] — [Thomas pricing](https://www.thomas.co/pricing). Thomas PPA in a recruiter proposal: £150 per candidate (≈ USD 201). — [ETL proposal](https://online.flippingbook.com/view/2999/13/)
- SHL: G-Cloud example subscriptions of £2,777-£4,195/yr for up to 100 candidates (≈ USD 3,700-5,600). "Unit" subscriptions start at £4,500 for 0-5,000 units. [PROCUREMENT/LIST, 2024 doc] — [SHL G-Cloud 14](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/712341/704579213621572-pricing-document-2024-05-03-1352.pdf)
- Scottish Government graduate testing: £19-£34.95 per candidate (2021, 2023). [PROCUREMENT] — [gov.scot FOI](https://www.gov.scot/publications/foi-202500456140/)
- Hogan single report: about USD 250 (reseller list); Candidate Summary USD 160. — [Coach Pathway Academy](https://coachpathwayacademy.com/product/hogan-insight-standalone-report-hpi-hds-or-mvpi/); [Performance Programs](https://performanceprograms.com/hogan/hogan-products/hogan-leadership/)
- Typical "background checks and assessment tools cost **$30 to $200 per candidate screened**". [3P-EST, Apr 2026] — [Teamed](https://www.teamed.global/insights/cost-per-hire-calculator-shrm-formula-and-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- For a 2-6 finalist shortlist, branded psychometrics alone cost about USD 300-2,400 in total (USD 150-400 × 2-6), before any interpretation. Interpretation by a certified practitioner is what pushes per-candidate cost to USD 1,000+ (see Section 1).

### Gaps
- No SHL per-candidate retail price was found; SHL sells by subscription.
- I found no Thomas International numeric list price on the vendor's own page.

## 3. Interview-as-a-service and structured interviewing / interview intelligence tools

### Takeaway
These are software subscriptions or high-volume technical-interview outsourcing, priced at **USD 3,600-175,000+ per year**. None sells a "per role" senior-finance product. The only per-interview price evidence is Karat, at about **USD 200-450 per technical interview**, which is an estimate.

- **Metaview**: the published page now prices AI sourcing at **USD 0 / 100 / 300 per month**, with Enterprise on quote. [LIST, accessed Oct 2026] — [Metaview pricing](https://www.metaview.ai/pricing). For interview notes, a third party reports a Pro plan at USD 50/user/month (annual). ### Inferences
- These tools are not substitutes for a one-off, per-role advisory. They need an annual commitment worth more than USD 6,000 (except Metaview's per-seat tiers) and they support interviewers rather than deliver a decision review.
- The internal panel cost estimate (about USD 1,000-1,900 per finalist session for senior panels) implies that a 4-finalist, 4-panelist final round consumes roughly USD 4,000-7,500 of internal time.

### Gaps
- I found no published per-interview price for any interview-as-a-service firm covering finance or non-technical roles.
- BrightHire, HireVue and Karat publish no list prices.

## 5. Fixed-fee / per-role hiring advisory, interview design, panel facilitation, decision support (boutiques)

### Takeaway
Boutique HR consultancies do publish fixed fees, mostly from the US and UK:
- **About USD 1,400** buys senior-hire panel participation (up to 10 candidates).
- **About USD 2,300-4,000** buys a diagnostic or a fixed-fee per-role process.
- **USD 4,000-12,000** buys interview-design projects.
- **USD 28,500** buys a multi-month turnkey interviewing playbook.

I found no published offer exactly matching "independent review of one shortlist plus panel decision support for one finance role". USD 6,000 sits in the middle-upper part of the published per-project range.

## 6. Per-hire assessment spend as a share of hire cost (SHRM, CIPD, Talent Board)

### Takeaway
SHRM's 2025 benchmarks put average cost-per-hire at **USD 5,475 (non-executive)** and **USD 35,879 (executive)**. I found no credible survey reporting assessment spend as a share of total hire cost. The only shares available are secondary or marketing claims: psychometrics add **2-5%**, or "ancillary" assessments plus travel add **5-10%**, to retained search fees.

### Cited Findings
- **SHRM 2025 Benchmarking Reports** (released 15 Oct 2025; more than 2,300 members surveyed Jan-Mar 2025): cost-per-hire **USD 5,475 nonexecutive** and **USD 35,879 executive**, "nearly 7x". [SURVEY] — [SHRM press release](https://www.shrm.org/about/press-room/shrm-releases-2025-benchmarking-reports--how-does-your-organizat); [SHRM State of Recruiting 2025](https://www.shrm.org/executive-network/insights/people-strategy/state-of-recruiting-2025-insights-to-maximize-recruitment)
- The SHRM CPH figure follows the ANSI/SHRM CPH standard. It includes external costs such as assessments, and excludes most interviewer panel time. [3P summary] — [InterviewCost SHRM summary](https://interviewcost.com/shrm-cost-per-hire)
- Retained search firms "layer additional charges for … psychometric assessments (**2-5%**)". [3P/marketing, undated] — [The Good Search](https://tgsus.com/executive-search-blog/executive-search-fees-search-firm-pricing/)
- "Ancillary charges (including assessments, travel, and extended guarantees) can add an average of **5-10%** to the base search fee for complex C-suite mandates". [3P/marketing, Jul 2026] — [JRG Partners](https://www.jrgpartners.com/executive-search-fees-industry-2026-pricing-benchmarks/)
- Teamed, role-band benchmarks:
  - "Manager and director positions run **$6,000 to $15,000**" per hire
  - VP+ "$15,000 to $50,000+"
  - "assessment tools cost $30 to $200 per candidate screened"
  - [3P-EST, Apr 2026] — [Teamed](https://www.teamed.global/insights/cost-per-hire-calculator-shrm-formula-and-benchmarks)
- SHRM 2025: only **20%** of organisations track quality of hire. [SURVEY via secondary] — [digidai summary of SHRM 2025](https://digidai.github.io/2025/12/18/recruitment-efficiency-benchmarks-industry-role-2025/index.md)

### Inferences
- A USD 6,000 per-role fee is about 1.1× SHRM's average non-executive cost-per-hire and about 17% of SHRM's average executive cost-per-hire.
- Against a UK council's executive-search alternative for a Director of Resources (about £14,000 fee plus £8,000 advertising, ≈ USD 29,500 total), USD 6,000 would be about 20%.
- If the 2-5% psychometric-add-on rule applied to a USD 97,000 Korn Ferry-average search, it would imply about USD 1,900-4,850 of assessment spend per executive search. That is below or near USD 6,000. This is an inference from secondary figures, not survey data.

### Gaps
- I found no SHRM, CIPD (Resourcing and Talent Planning) or Talent Board figure for assessment spend per hire or as a percentage of hiring cost. The CIPD's latest resourcing survey was not retrieved.
- There is no GCC-specific cost-per-hire benchmark in this pass.

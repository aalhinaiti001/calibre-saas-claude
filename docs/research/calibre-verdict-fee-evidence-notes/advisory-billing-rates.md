# Advisory billing rates: UAE, KSA, Jordan (and a UK anchor), 2023–2026

Research date: 5 Oct 2026. Benchmark under test: USD 350 per all-in founder hour (about USD 2,800 per 8-hour day), and a fixed fee of USD 6,000 for roughly 15–17 hours of senior work.

**Conversion rates used:** AED 3.6725/USD, SAR 3.75/USD, JOD 0.709/USD (pegs, as instructed). GBP: £1 = USD 1.3212, the Bank of England daily spot rate for 1 Oct 2026 ([BoE](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/boeapps/database/Rates.asp)). For context, GBP/USD averaged about 1.341 in September 2026 ([ExchangeRates.org.uk](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/GBP-USD-spot-exchange-rates-history-2026.html)). Unless a line says otherwise, hourly equivalents assume an 8-hour day.

**The benchmark in each currency:** USD 350/hr = AED 1,285/hr = SAR 1,313/hr = JOD 248/hr = about £265/hr. USD 2,800/day = AED 10,283 = SAR 10,500 = JOD 1,985 = about £2,119. The USD 6,000 fixed fee works out at USD 400/hr over 15 hours and USD 353/hr over 17 hours. In other currencies it is AED 22,035, SAR 22,500, JOD 4,254 or about £4,541.

**Evidence grading used below:**
- [RATE CARD]: published government or framework rate.
- [SURVEY]: a survey with a stated sample.
- [FIRM PRICE GUIDE]: a vendor's guide to market prices. These are marketing material and the vendor has an interest in the figures.
- [AGGREGATOR]: an SEO or benchmark site that does not show its method.
- [ANECDOTE]: a single job ad, post or quote.

## Q1. Big 4 and mid-tier hourly or daily rates in the UAE and KSA by grade; public-sector rate cards or tender disclosures

### Takeaway
I found no published Big 4 or mid-tier rate card for the UAE or KSA by grade. I also found no Etimad or UAE tender disclosure of rates by grade, and no public-sector framework rate card. The GCC figures that exist come from vendor or aggregator websites. They put Big 4 partners at roughly USD 800–2,000+/hr, which is several times the USD 350 benchmark, but this evidence is weak. Published US federal schedules for MBB senior partners are about USD 1,100–1,200/hr (2024).

### Cited Findings
- **SKYLINE Industrial Solutions, KSA (IT consulting vendor), Feb 2026** [FIRM PRICE GUIDE, treat as suspect]. It gives these "realistic 2026 ranges before discounting": Big 4 partner SAR 5,000–8,000/hr (USD 1,333–2,133/hr), Big 4 senior manager SAR 3,200–4,500/hr (USD 853–1,200/hr), boutique or freelance SAR 600–1,500/hr (USD 160–400/hr), and its own specialist rate SAR 1,000–1,500/hr (USD 267–400/hr).   - The same page also gives a different Big 4 band in a table, SAR 3,800–7,500/hr. The partner figures are above the US federal MBB schedules below, so treat them as an outlier marketing claim.
- **Selectra Strategy, "GCC Consulting Market Report 2026"** [FIRM PRICE GUIDE; the author sells an independent-consultant network]. It says a senior partner at a top-tier strategy firm "typically bills at $800 to $1,500 per hour". - **Cost basis, Tenure Careers** [AGGREGATOR]. Big Four consulting partner pay in the region is AED 1.5M–2.2M base (USD 410,000–600,000) plus a 150–250% bonus, for USD 700k–1.5M total. Big Four senior manager base is AED 350,000–480,000. — [tenurecareers.io, 23 Mar 2026](https://tenurecareers.io/insights/consulting-salaries-gcc-mbb-big4-2026)

- **Saudi market price pressure** [news].   - AGBI reports the Saudi consulting market grew 18.2% in 2023 to USD 3.2bn, and that consultants now have to "prove their worth". — [AGBI, 8 Jan 2025](https://www.agbi.com/analysis/giga-projects/2025/01/end-of-blank-cheque-culture-for-saudi-consultancy/)
- **Saudi government consulting freeze, May 2026** [news]. Government entities, including PIF, were told to pause payments to and new contracts with strategy, management-consulting and law firms until about the end of June 2026. The Ministry of Finance disputed this characterisation. — [The National, 21 May 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/2026/05/21/saudi-arabia-freezes-payments-to-consultancies-as-it-feels-effects-of-iran-war/); [Consultancy-me, 22 May 2026](https://www.consultancy-me.com/news/13502/saudi-government-freezes-and-delays-consulting-spend-amid-war-driven-fiscal-tightening)
- **Saudi EXPRO (Expenditure and Projects Efficiency Authority)** runs a "Government Procurement Indicative Pricing Tool" with price databases and cost calculators for government procurement. It also published a cost-estimation manual and a guidebook on consultancy RFPs (Q3 2024). No consulting rates by grade are publicly visible. — [EXPRO pricing tool](https://expro.gov.sa/en/services/cost); [EXPRO manuals news](https://expro.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/expro-launches-7-government-procurement-manuals)
- **Consultancy-me.com** says "there is relatively little information available publicly on the fees and rates charged by consultants". It gives only tiers of revenue per consultant: Big Four and global functional specialists at about USD 300,000–400,000 per consultant per year. — [Consultancy-me fees & rates page (undated)](https://www.consultancy-me.com/consulting-industry/fees-rates)

### Inferences
- Even at the low end of these unreliable GCC claims, Big 4 and MBB partner time is 2.5–6x the USD 350 benchmark. A Big 4 senior manager would also cost more than USD 350/hr at standard rates if the SKYLINE or Selectra claims hold. USD 350/hr is therefore well below what a large firm would charge for comparable seniority.
- A cost-based cross-check gives the same picture. Big 4 GCC partner total pay of USD 0.7–1.5M a year, at about 1,200–1,400 billable hours and a usual 2.5–4x cost-to-charge multiple, implies a charge-out of well over USD 1,000/hr. This is my inference and is not published.

### Gaps
- No Etimad (KSA) or UAE federal or emirate tender award discloses consulting rates by grade. Etimad award notices give contract values but not rate cards, and I found none that could be parsed.
- No Big 4 or mid-tier (BDO, Grant Thornton, Crowe, RSM) GCC grade rate card is published. All GCC Big 4 figures above come from interested vendors or aggregators.
- I did not obtain Source Global Research's paid GCC pricing data.

## Q2. Independent and boutique senior consultants, fractional CFOs and interim finance directors in the UAE, KSA and Jordan

### Takeaway
In the UAE, fractional CFO retainers are published at about AED 15,000–60,000/month (USD 4,100–16,300) for 10–40 hours a month. Interim and fractional CFO day rates are published at about AED 3,000–9,000/day (USD 820–2,450). Senior independent advisers are quoted at AED 5,000–10,000+/day (USD 1,360–2,720+). USD 2,800/day is at or just above the top of these published UAE bands. USD 350/hr sits within the "independent senior" band of USD 300–500/hr claimed for the GCC. I found almost no published Jordan data, and what exists suggests much lower local rates.

### Inferences
- **Implied UAE fractional CFO hourly rate.** Matching the CFO Centre's AED 15k–60k/month retainer band to its 10–40 hours a month gives about AED 1,500/hr (USD 408/hr) at both ends. The pairing is my inference; the source does not link the two ranges. The full arithmetic range is much wider, AED 375–6,000/hr. USD 350/hr is close to this implied mid-point.
- **Day-rate comparison (UAE).**
  - USD 2,800/day (AED 10,283) is just above FD Capital's top interim CFO rate (AED 9,000) and at the top of Omnispay's "senior specialist" band (AED 10,000+).
  - Interim and fractional day rates are for multi-day, embedded work. A short, discrete advisory engagement of about 2 days normally carries a premium per hour. So USD 350/hr is defensible for a senior advisor at the top of the independent market. It is not a mid-market rate.
- **Jordan.** USD 350/hr is far above any published or indicative local Jordanian rate; even the high end of USD 350/day is one-eighth of USD 2,800/day. The benchmark only holds for Jordan-based clients if they are paying for GCC- or international-calibre advice.

### Gaps
- No Michael Page, Odgers Interim, Interim Partners or Hays Middle East interim day-rate survey was found published for 2023–2026.
- No published fractional CFO rates were found for KSA (Riyadh) specifically.
- No published senior advisory or CFO rates were found for Jordan. Donor (USAID, EU, GIZ) daily fee caps for senior local experts were not found in accessible form.

## Q3. HR, people-advisory and organisational consultant fees in the region

### Takeaway
Regional HR consultancy is mostly priced by project or retainer, not by the hour. Published KSA project fees are SAR 25,000–90,000 (USD 6,700–24,000) and retainers start at about SAR 12,000/month (USD 3,200). Low-end HR outsourcing "consulting projects" are SAR 5,000–15,000. I found no published GCC hourly or day rate for senior HR advisers. Western benchmarks put senior HR specialists at about USD 200–400/hr.

### Cited Findings
- **Talent Arabia (GCC HR consultancy serving KSA)** [FIRM PRICE GUIDE]. "Single-project engagements such as a Nitaqat optimisation study or HR policy refresh run SAR 25,000 to 90,000 (USD 6,667–24,000). Multi-month retainers start around SAR 12,000 per month (USD 3,200)." It sends a "fixed-fee proposal within three business days". — [talentarabia.com/hr-consultancy-saudi-arabia (undated, current site)](https://www.talentarabia.com/hr-consultancy-saudi-arabia)
- **360 Solutions (KSA HR outsourcing), May 2026** [FIRM PRICE GUIDE]. HR consulting (project) for restructuring or policy development: SAR 5,000–15,000 per project (USD 1,333–4,000). HR outsourcing runs SAR 2,500–15,000+/month by company size. — [hr360s.com, 20 May 2026](https://hr360s.com/en/how-much-does-hr-outsourcing-cost/)
- **Motaded (KSA HR/PRO packages)** [published price list]: SAR 1,750–3,150 per employee per month for HR administration packages. This is transactional HR, not advisory, and is useful only as a floor reference. — [motaded.com.sa/hr-packages](https://motaded.com.sa/hr-packages)
- **Western HR consultant benchmarks, Jan 2026** [AGGREGATOR]. Experienced HR consultants in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland and New Zealand charge about USD 100–200/hr, and senior specialists or complex cases often USD 200–400/hr. UK SME-oriented HR consultants charge about £500–750/day (USD 661–991). — [getmorehrclients.com, 16 Jan 2026](https://getmorehrclients.com/blog/hr-consultant-pricing-in-2026/)
- **Omnispay (UAE)** says monthly retainers are common for "ongoing marketing, finance, HR, strategy or advisory support", but gives no HR-specific figure. — [omnispay.com, 5 Sep 2026](https://omnispay.com/blog/consultant-fees-uae)

### Inferences
- In the KSA SME segment, HR project fees of SAR 25,000–90,000 bracket the USD 6,000 (SAR 22,500) fixed fee from just below. USD 6,000 for a 15–17-hour piece of senior hiring or people advice is therefore below a typical full HR project price but in the same order of magnitude. It is also above low-end outsourcer "projects" of SAR 5–15k.
- USD 350/hr for senior HR or hiring advice is at the top of the Western senior-specialist band (USD 200–400/hr). It is easier to justify when bundled with finance or board-level hiring judgement than as stand-alone HR advice.

### Gaps
- No published hourly or day rates were found for senior HR or organisational-design consultants in the UAE, KSA or Jordan. No Mercer, Korn Ferry, WTW or Big 4 People Advisory GCC rates are public.
- No Jordan HR consultancy pricing was found.

## Q4. GCC versus UK rates at the same seniority (UK anchor)

### Takeaway
UK independent and boutique senior rates (2026) are about £1,000–3,500/day (USD 1,320–4,620). UK interim CFOs have a median of £1,200/day (USD 1,585), and the 90th percentile is £2,000/day (USD 2,640). USD 2,800/day (about £2,119) is above the 90th percentile of UK interim CFO day rates. It is mid-range for UK independent or boutique senior consultants and below UK Big 4 partner rates (£3,500–6,000/day). The UAE figures above (AED 4,000–10,000/day, USD 1,090–2,720) broadly overlap the UK interim and independent ranges. I found no rigorous like-for-like GCC versus UK comparison.

### Inferences
- Against the UK anchor, USD 350/hr (about £265/hr, or £2,119/day) is:
  - above the UK interim CFO 90th percentile (£2,000/day);
  - inside the UK "independent partner/director" band (£1,500–3,500/day) and the lower half of the boutique-partner band (£2,500–5,000/day);
  - well below Big 4 partner rates (£3,500–6,000/day).
- So the benchmark is "top-of-market independent / lower boutique-partner" in UK terms. It is not "interim" pricing, and it is not "Big 4" pricing.

### Gaps
- MCF3/MCF4 maximum day rates by grade could not be retrieved. The FOI PDFs and the GCA filtering tool were not accessible.
- No source directly compares GCC and UK billing rates for the same grade.

## Q5. Fixed-fee or productised advisory pricing norms and client preference in the GCC SME and family-business segment

### Takeaway
Regional vendors consistently describe retainers as the dominant model for ongoing work and fixed fees as the norm for defined-scope projects. They describe hourly billing as poorly trusted by GCC buyers. Globally, Source Global Research finds the split between time-and-materials, fixed-price and outcome-based contracts changes only slowly. Fewer than 2% of fees are truly at risk. GCC buyers in 2025–2026 are increasingly focused on ROI and price. I found no survey that specifically measures fixed-fee preference among GCC SMEs or family businesses.

### Cited Findings
- **The Growth Consultants (GCC B2B advisory), Apr 2026** [opinion / marketing]:
  - "Time-based billing (hourly or daily rates) is poorly understood and poorly trusted in markets where outcome is the primary value currency."
  - "Retainer — the dominant commercial model for professional services in the GCC."
  - A fixed fee per project is "clean, trustworthy, easy to approve internally".
  - GCC buyers "expect to negotiate", and a fixed rate card "signals inflexibility". — [thegrowthconsultants.biz, 25 Apr 2026](https://thegrowthconsultants.biz/blogs/pricing-commercial-models-gcc.html)
- **JaZaa (UAE), 2026** [FIRM PRICE GUIDE]. The retainer is "the most common for ongoing work". Project fees "give cost certainty for a defined piece of work". Hourly billing "discourages the quick question" and suits only narrow, ad-hoc needs. — [jazaa.com](https://jazaa.com/blog/fractional-cfo-cost-uae-dubai/)
- **Omnispay (UAE), Sep 2026.** Fixed fees "work better when everyone knows the deliverable" (example: AED 30,000 market-entry study). Retainers suit ongoing finance and HR support. — [omnispay.com](https://omnispay.com/blog/consultant-fees-uae)

### Inferences
- A fixed fee for a defined deliverable is consistent with the pricing GCC SME and family-business buyers are said to prefer. Quoting USD 350/hr openly is likely to invite negotiation. A fixed USD 6,000 for a named output is the more market-consistent presentation, and the hourly rate can stay an internal check.
- The USD 6,000 fee implies USD 353–400/hr for 15–17 hours, i.e. at or slightly above the USD 350 benchmark. In absolute terms it is at the low end of published UAE and KSA project fees for comparable advisory work:
  - UAE fractional-CFO projects: AED 25,000–150,000 (USD 6,800–40,800) and AED 30,000–80,000 (USD 8,200–21,800).
  - KSA HR projects: SAR 25,000–90,000 (USD 6,700–24,000).
  - UAE example market-entry study: AED 30,000 (USD 8,200).
  
  So a client comparing against these published project prices would see USD 6,000 as modest, even though the implied hourly rate is senior.
- The softening Saudi market in 2025–2026 (discounting, the government payment freeze, ROI scrutiny) argues against pricing much above USD 350/hr for KSA clients now. It does not argue for going below it.

### Gaps
- No survey isolates fixed-fee preference among GCC SMEs or family businesses. Source's GCC samples are large enterprises (94% above USD 500m revenue).
- No published data shows typical hours behind GCC fixed-fee advisory projects, so implied hourly rates for the published project fees cannot be derived.
- The Consultancy-me Jul 2024 article on deliverable-led pricing was not read.

### Overall bottom line for the report writer (inference, built from the cited findings above)
- **In the UAE and KSA,** USD 350/hr (USD 2,800/day) is at the top of published independent and fractional senior finance ranges:
  - UAE interim and fractional CFO rates of AED 3,000–9,000/day top out at about USD 2,450/day.
  - Senior independent advisers are quoted at AED 5,000–10,000+/day, topping out at about USD 2,720+.
  - The implied UAE fractional CFO rate is about USD 400/hr (my inference from the CFO Centre ranges).
  - The claimed GCC independent senior band is USD 300–500/hr.
  - It is far below Big 4 and MBB partner rates, which are not published for the GCC; vendor claims put them at USD 800–2,000+/hr.
- **In UK terms,** it equals about £2,119/day. That is above the interim CFO 90th percentile (£2,000) and within the independent senior and boutique partner bands.
- **In Jordan,** it is far above any local evidence, and the benchmark only holds for Jordan clients if the advice is GCC- or international-calibre.
- **On the fixed fee,** USD 6,000 for 15–17 hours implies USD 353–400/hr. That is consistent with the benchmark and below most published GCC project fees in absolute terms.
- **Evidence quality:** nearly all GCC figures are vendor price guides or aggregators, not rate cards or surveys. The only true surveys found are UK-based (Holdsway, n=200; IIM).

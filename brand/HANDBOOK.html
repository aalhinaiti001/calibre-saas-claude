§ 01 · Purpose

Positioning boundaries, target market limitations, and the go-to-market progression sequence.

Narrow finance-hiring advisory. Nothing wider.

Calibre evaluates employer-provided finalist shortlists for complex corporate finance roles using independent reading, anchored scorecards, and human calibration. The employer makes and owns the decision.

What Calibre Is

  • An anchored, evidence-based review of finalist candidates for a specific finance mandate.
  • Independent scoring by calibrated reviewers against custom-anchored rubrics.
  • A written executive memo explaining the qualitative evidence, limits, and risks.
  • Commercial structure: One role · two weeks · fixed fee.

What Calibre Is Not

  • Not recruitment, headhunting, or candidate sourcing.
  • Not a psychometric test, personality profile, or cognitive battery.
  • Not a statistical predictor of executive performance or cultural fit.
  • Not an automated, algorithmic, or software-driven sorting engine.

The Finance-Only Boundary

The evaluation method rests entirely on rigorous finance judgement: close ownership, internal financial controls, capital allocation, statutory accounting, and technical reporting. Applied outside of finance functions, the scorecard rubrics lose objective anchoring. Acknowledging this boundary publicly is the core mechanism of Calibre’s brand authority.

Go-To-Market Lifecycle: Attach → Prove → Separate

§ 04 · Governance

The zero-tolerance claim filter. Prohibited vocabulary, risk boundaries, and mandatory legal copy.

The Zero-Tolerance Claim Gate.

Calibre never promises a ranking, a predictive score, or an automated hiring conclusion. Every sentence must clear this lexicographical filter prior to deployment.

Prohibited Lexicon & Mandatory Substitutions
Prohibited Trigger TermSubstantive ViolationMandatory Approved Substitute
Rank / Ranked / Ranking / Top / Winner Implies candidates are arranged on an ordinal ladder. Calibre presents finalists exclusively in entry order. "One written recommendation detailing comparative merits and verified risks."
Fit / Fit Score / Role-Fit / Best Fit Implies psychometric matching or universal benchmarking against an undefined aggregate standard. "Evidence scored against explicit, anchored criteria built for the role."
Diagnostic Retired commercial terminology suggesting medical or clinical precision. "The Calibre Verdict" or "Hiring decision advisory memo."
Predict / Predictive / Validity Legal liability trigger. Calibre does not conduct predictive validity tracking. "Structured evaluation of past performance evidence."
Assessment / Psychometric Mischaracterizes advisory consulting work as a standardized testing platform. "Structured hiring advisory for finance roles."
Unsourced Stats (e.g. "Δ 41 pts") Unsubstantiated marketing claims undermine product credibility. Traceable client citations or explicit "Illustrative example" labels.
Mandatory 4-Sentence Use Statement

The following statement must be quoted in full on every web footer, proposal document, and executive memo. Truncating the statement voids compliance:

"Structured hiring advisory for finance roles. Not a psychometric assessment. Does not predict performance. The decision, and its consequences, rest with the employer."

Mandatory Claim Phrasing

  • "Scored independently against a role-specific standard."
  • "Finalists presented in entry order."
  • "One written recommendation, with the reasons and risks named."
  • "Human calibration across independent reviewers."
  • "Evidence-led evaluation of technical finance competence."

Strictly Prohibited Copy

  • "Identifies the top-ranked candidate for your team."
  • "Predicts 12-month job performance with high validity."
  • "Delivers an automated candidate fit rating."
  • "Eliminates hiring risk through psychometric diagnostics."
  • "Ranks your shortlist from strongest to weakest."
§ 05 · Deliverables

Operational anatomy of the Calibre Verdict memo and core evaluation mechanics.

The Calibre Verdict Deliverable.

The primary client output is a single, rigorous, human-written decision memo. It provides structured clarity without stripping final accountability from executive management.

Core Deliverable Architecture

Deliverable Components
Output AssetAnalytical FunctionFormat Standard
Role Definition Matrix Explicit documentation of performance expectations, reporting lines, and non-negotiable finance competencies. Tabular breakdown across technical, compliance, and leadership requirements.
Anchored Scorecard Custom rubric detailing observable evidence benchmarks for scoring candidate submissions. Graded on a 1–5 evidence scale with descriptive behavioral anchors.
Independent Review Grid Disaggregated candidate ratings from primary readers, scored without mutual consultation. Finalists displayed in entry order. Variance highlighted to expose divergence.
Calibration Notes Documentation of the consensus session where reviewer divergence is analyzed and resolved. Prose narrative explaining why reviewers agreed or disagreed on specific work samples.
Executive Verdict Memo The core memorandum: synthesizes evidence, highlights operational risks, and delivers the recommendation. Typeset in Lora serif on white ground. Features the full mandatory use statement.

Review Mechanics & The Entry-Order Principle

  • Entry Order Standard: Finalists are listed in the order their dossiers were received. They are never sorted by aggregate score, color-coded green/amber/red, or designated with competitive badges ("Finalist Winner").
  • Mandatory Risk Disclosure: If a candidate's portfolio reveals thin documentation regarding specific technical competencies (e.g., debt covenants, statutory consolidation), the memo must explicitly name the omission as an unresolved risk. Silence is prohibited.
  • Human Authorship: Verdict memos are written by a named human author. Generative synthesis, score-driven template autofill, and algorithmic recommendations are strictly barred.

Grammatical Correction Checkpoint (The Dual-Verb Mandate)

The previous Arabic footer contained a grammatical error in the liability disclaimer:
القرار وتبعاته تبقى على عاتق صاحب العمل (Incorrect feminine singular verb).
Correct Canonical Standard: القرار وتبعاته يبقيان على عاتق صاحب العمل (Dual subject requires dual verb). This correction is mandatory across all digital and print collateral.

Accessibility & Contrast Benchmarks

  • Color Contrast: Body copy against ground must exceed WCAG AAA standards (7.0:1). Forest 600 (#265147) on White achieves 8.9:1; Ink 950 (#0E1113) achieves 18.9:1; Stone 600 (#394740) body text achieves 9.7:1. Stone 500 (#596B66) achieves 5.6:1, which is AA only, so it is restricted to secondary text and must not carry body copy.
  • Screen Reader Optimization: All decorative elements (monogram tile, structural dividers, icons) must carry aria-hidden="true". Informative diagrams require explicit semantic markup.
  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive elements must maintain a visible focus ring using outline: 2px solid var(--forest-600) with a 2px offset.

§ 08 · Governance

Pre-publish quality verification gates and the official register of quarantined operational decisions.

Production Release Gate & Decision Log.

Prior to pushing code to production or delivering collateral to a client, all ten verification gates must be certified by the brand custodian.

  • Attribution Seam: "by Daftar" kicker is present in sans-serif; Daftar mark and cream/rust palette are absent.
  • Color System Integrity: Ground is pure White (#FFFFFF); structural accents use Forest 600 (#265147); no charcoal theme overrides.
  • Typographic Stack: Lora and Plus Jakarta Sans active; zero monospaced styles or legacy type imports.
  • Zero-Tolerance Claim Pass: Content audited for banned vocabulary (rank, fit, diagnostic, predict).
  • Mandatory Use Statement: Full 4-sentence statement included verbatim without omissions.
  • Data Citation: Every numeric performance claim has a verified source or is clearly tagged "Illustrative".
  • Bilingual Parity: English and Arabic surfaces synchronized; dual-verb fix verified in Arabic.
  • Technical Hygiene: #tweakPanel stripped; Tailwind compiled locally; reduced-motion CSS intact.
  • Data Privacy: Candidate names, client identifiers, and confidential finance metrics fully sanitized.
  • Operational Alignment: Scope strictly conforms to "One role · two weeks · fixed fee".
